About Me

Hi, this is Viviene!

I’m a Data Analyst with experience in insurance, insurtech, and technology research, specializing in claims analytics, dashboards design, market research, and system optimization. I'm passionate about turning complex data into clear insights that improve operational efficiency and business performance.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Applied Mathematics and a Bachelor of Arts in Computer Science from Brandeis University, where I built a strong foundation in statistics, programming, data modeling, and machine learning. My academic and project work equipped me to solve real-world business problems through quantitative analysis and structured thinking.

As a Data Analyst at FWD Insurance, I analyzed large-scale claim and customer data to improve customer experience and streamline the claim settlement process. I also designed multi-layer data models, built executive dashboards, and translated analytical findings into business insights that identified severity-driven risk, optimized claims operations, and surfaced potential fraud signals.

In my portfolio below, I'd like to share some of my projects that I found interesting. Hope you enjoy reading it!
