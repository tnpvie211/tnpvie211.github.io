About Me

Hi, this is Viviene!

I am currently a solutions data analyst at FWD Insurance. I have 4+ years experience in bridging business and technical teams, solving complex problems, and helping turn ideas into impactful products across InsurTech, FinTech, Healthcare IT, and Enterprise Systems. Throughout my career, I have been drawn to one recurring challenge: helping organizations make better decisions by turning business problems into practical, data-driven solutions.

At FWD Insurance Group, I led strategy presentations for C-suite executives, initiated AI proof-of-concept projects, built data frameworks, and developed analytics solutions that support decision-making across claims, pricing, customer experience, and risk management.

Prior to FWD, I partnered with financial institutions at InvestCloud to implement SaaS wealth management platforms, translating business requirements into scalable system and data solutions while supporting integrations and end-to-end project delivery. Earlier in my career, I contributed to healthcare digital transformation initiatives, focusing on large-scale EHR data migration, vendor management, and workflow optimization.

In my portfolio below, I'd like to share some of my projects that I found interesting. Hope you enjoy reading it!

Business Analytics & Data Science Projects

Auto Insurance Claim & Risk Analysis

An auto insurance company was facing a puzzling situation: claim volumes had remained relatively stable, yet profitability continued to decline. Leadership needed to understand what was driving the deterioration in financial performance. This project proposed and tested multiple hypotheses to uncover the key factors impacting profit margins.

(Python, MySQL, Tableau)

  • Tableau

Boston Airbnb Market & Pricing Analysis

A Boston homeowner was considering entering the Airbnb market but faced a common challenge: determining the right price to attract guests while maximizing revenue. This project examined the local Airbnb landscape, identifying how seasonality, neighborhood, property characteristics, and amenities influence pricing. Even better, a machine learning model was built to help hosts estimate competitive prices for new listings.

(Python, Pandas, Numpy, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Ridge, Lasso, Linear Regression)

  • GitHub

Student Learning Pattern Analysis & Prediction

An online learning platform wanted to better understand why some students thrive while others struggle. With access to large volumes of learning interaction data, the company sought insights into student behavior, engagement, and academic performance. This project analyzed learning patterns, built predictive models to identify at-risk students, and generated recommendations for delivering more personalized learning experiences.

(Python, MySQL, LightBGM, XGBoost, Neural Keras)

Data Engineering & Applied Machine Learning Projects

Multi-Source ETL Crawling To Reporting Pipeline

This project implements an end-to-end enterprise ETL and reporting system for insurance operations. It ingests data from multiple internal sources, standardizes schemas, maintains historical snapshots, validates data quality, and generates automated analytical and regulatory reports.

(Python, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Elasticsearch, SQLAlchemy, Logstash)

  • GitHub

Time-Series Stock Forecasting

Retrieves historical stock price data, cleans, normalizes it, and builds predictive models to forecast future stock prices. It compares a baseline Linear Regression model with a more advanced LSTM deep learning model to demonstrate how time-series modeling improves prediction performance on financial data.

(Python, APIs, Linear Regression, LSTM, Keras/TensorFlow)

  • GitHub

API-Based Restaurant Data Enrichment

Python-based data ingestion pipeline to extract and enrich restaurant metadata from the Yelp REST API, integrating third-party business attributes into an existing dataset for downstream analytics and visualization.
